## Synopsis (Background)

The **Kootenay Lightweb Community Services Cooperative** is a working proof that technology can become a bridge to awakening when communities own it outright. We are a registered co-op running real production infrastructure: a 4-node Proxmox/Ceph/Kubernetes cluster called **Liberty**, hosting Nextcloud (144+ active users), Matrix/Synapse, Jitsi, Keycloak SSO, Gitea, Discourse, and our own mail at koots.net.

Our recent Visionary Fund crowdfund closed at **103% of goal** with **80 contributors** and **$8,785 CAD raised**, with hardware budget already earmarked for a Kaslo expansion that extends the federation deeper into the bioregion.

What sits underneath the stack is a philosophy. The Lightweb is creative civil action expressed in code — Buckminster Fuller's principle in practice.

- Where many communities argue about Big Tech, we crowdfunded eight thousand dollars and built our own cloud.
- Where many petition for privacy law, we deployed a Matrix homeserver and invited our friends.
- Where many talk about ethical AI, we run it locally on our own hardware behind Ollama.

The infrastructure exists. The next phase is articulation, scale, and the magnetic public presence the work deserves.

This 2-hour session is the first formal working meeting between the Lightweb and your AI systems. The aim is to load you and your AI stack with the foundational context of the project — the architecture, the philosophy, the people, the resources, the soul code — so AI can generate strategic assets in parallel with the human work already underway. The AGM is the next major milestone, and what we shape together here becomes the asset set we walk into that room with.

## The Strategic Frame

The Lightweb uses technology to strengthen offline community, local trust, and real-world resilience. It is locally rooted and globally connectable, privacy-conscious, decentralized, and human-centered.

Opting out of AI entirely remains a fully supported choice inside the Lightweb. Our AI work focuses on **Good AI**: community-owned, renewable-powered, running on repurposed hardware, aligned with local values, distinct from the surveillance AI of Big Tech.

### The big question for the session

> If the Lightweb became wildly successful in five years, what would everyday people in the Kootenays and beyond actually be using every week — and why would they love it?

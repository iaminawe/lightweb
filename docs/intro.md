# Kootenay Lightweb

Planning and reference material for the **Kootenay Lightweb Community Services Cooperative** — a registered co-op running production infrastructure on the *Liberty* Proxmox/Ceph/Kubernetes cluster.

> **We are not rejecting the Internet — we are building one that belongs to its community.**
>
> *Sovereign tech for the awakening mind.*

The full frame lives in **[Philosophy](./philosophy)**. If a decision feels hard, that's the page to come back to first.

## The strategic frame in one breath

Every design moves through a federated scale model — **individual → group → community → regional → global** — with local control held at every layer. The single product-decision test:

> *Does this strengthen offline trust and local resilience, or pull people deeper into screens?*

Three commitments run through everything else:

- **Good AI** is community-owned, opt-in, transparent, renewable-powered. **Opting out of AI is fully supported.** See [Philosophy → Good AI](./philosophy#good-ai--distinct-from-surveillance-ai).
- **A threshold, not a wall.** Membership is **vouched-in** via a double-referral graph — human verification through community trust, not surveillance identity. See [Philosophy → A threshold, not a wall](./philosophy#a-threshold-not-a-wall).
- **Replication-ready.** What's built here is a *pattern* other bioregions can stand up against their own Liberty-equivalent. See [Philosophy → The federated scale model](./philosophy#the-federated-scale-model).

## What this handbook is for

This is the **public** handbook — the philosophy, the strategy, the architecture, the tooling picks. It is written so another bioregion could read it and stand up the pattern.

Private board material — concrete financials, member counts, named partnership pipelines, the pitch decks — lives outside this handbook by design.

That separation is itself a philosophical choice. See [Philosophy → The truth rises](./philosophy#the-truth-rises).
